# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lifestory is for families and clients who purchase a legacy-preservation package. They use the product to see, maintain, and revisit a family's preserved story through an interactive family tree, archive material, biographies, gallery assets, and related account flows. Their context is personal and emotionally sensitive: they may be exploring family history, updating details, sharing memories, or reviewing a premium service deliverable with relatives.

## Product Purpose

Lifestory preserves family memory across generations by pairing premium biography work with a secure digital family hub. The product should make family stories feel cared for, durable, and easy to return to, while giving clients confidence that names, relationships, media, and memories are organized with respect. Success means a family can understand its lineage, revisit preserved materials, and continue updating the family record without the interface getting in the way.

## Brand Personality

Legacy, minimalist, warm.

The voice should feel calm, respectful, and assured. It should carry emotional weight without becoming sentimental or theatrical, and should communicate premium care without feeling cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid anything that reads as a generic AI-generated template: overused SaaS patterns, decorative card grids, generic hero-metric blocks, gradient text, excessive glass effects, ornamental clutter, and visual flourishes that do not support the family's story or the user's task.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve before performing: every visual and interaction choice should make the family story easier to understand, trust, and revisit.
2. Warmth through restraint: use quiet materials, careful typography, and humane copy instead of excessive decoration.
3. Premium means cared for: details such as motion, empty states, focus states, and touch behavior should feel deliberate and stable.
4. The tree is the artifact: navigation, editing, search, and member detail flows should keep lineage and relationships clear at all times.
5. Emotional content needs calm tools: avoid abrupt feedback, noisy UI, or surprising motion when users are working with personal family material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Text contrast, focus visibility, keyboard access, reduced-motion behavior, form errors, touch targets, and responsive layouts should be treated as product requirements. The app should remain usable for families reviewing content on both desktop and mobile, including users who need clear labels, readable contrast, and predictable interactions.
